Hub page stand-in

This page plays the part of a hub page, to try the quick reference before it goes on the real hub. It loads one line: <script src="launcher.js" defer></script>.

Press Alt+M to open the mezmerize quick reference in its own window. Drag it anywhere. In it, P docks it into a panel on this page, and P again pops it back out. Alt+M or ESC closes it.
